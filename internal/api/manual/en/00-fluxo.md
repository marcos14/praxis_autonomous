# The lifecycle of a demand

The cycle of a demand is:

**PRD arrives → The analyst reads the code → You answer questions → Plan in phases → You approve → Runs in the worktree → You integrate.**

Everything else is automatic. You only step in at three moments: **answering the questions**, **approving the plan** and **integrating (merge)**. The card on the kanban tells you when it is your turn — and so do the notifications.
