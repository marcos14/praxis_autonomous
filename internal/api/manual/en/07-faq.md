# Frequently asked questions

- **Can two demands touch the same file?** They can — each one on its own branch. The conflict shows up at merge time; the kanban shows an overlap warning beforehand.
- **Where is the data stored?** Everything lives in the Praxis database (outside the projects): PRD, chat, questions, plans, phases, costs and logs. In the projects, only the commits on the branches.
- **What about cost?** Every demand has a budget cap. Once it is exceeded → it pauses and warns you. The Home screen shows spending per day and per project.
- **A run blew the budget and the demand failed. Did I lose everything?** No. Raise the engine budget (**Motores** / Engines screen) and click **Tentar novamente** (Retry) on the card — the demand resumes from the stage that failed (analysis, planning or the interrupted phase).
